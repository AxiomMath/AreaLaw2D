# Area law for the dimer / EPR-pair state

A Lean 4 formalization of the opening picture of Brandão–Horodecki,
[*"Entanglement Area Law from Exponential Decay of Correlations"*,
arXiv:1309.3789](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.3789),
Fig. 1a, plus a library layer for the mathematics of the full version,
[arXiv:1206.2947](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2947), which is the formalization target.

```lean
theorem Arealaw.dimer_area_law (n : ℕ) : EE (chain n).M = Real.log 2
```

A chain of `n` dimers has the same cut entropy as a single dimer: entanglement scales with the
boundary, here the single bond crossing the cut, not with the volume `n`.

## How it maps to Fig. 1a

Fig. 1a is a one-dimensional chain of singlet pairs cut in two. Bonds straddling the cut each
contribute one ebit; bonds entirely on one side contribute nothing.

| Paper | Here |
|---|---|
| Bell / EPR pair `(\|00⟩+\|11⟩)/√2` | `bell = (1/√2) • 1`, packaged as `bellState` |
| Bond straddling the cut | `bellState`, one qubit each side, `EE = log 2` |
| Dimer entirely on one side | `rightDimer`, cut side the singleton `Fin 1`, `EE = 0` |
| Chain of `n` dimers across the cut | `chain n = bellState ⊗ₖ (n interior dimers)` |
| Area law | `dimer_area_law : ∀ n, EE (chain n) = log 2` |

## Definitions

Stated over a general `RCLike` field `𝕜`; only the concrete `bell` and `chain` are fixed to `ℂ`.

- Shannon entropy of a finite real family: `H p = ∑ i, Real.negMulLog (p i)`.
- Bipartite pure state: a coefficient matrix `M : Matrix A B 𝕜` with `∑ a b, ‖M a b‖² = 1`.
- Reduced density operator: `rhoA M = M * Mᴴ`, Hermitian of trace one.
- Entanglement entropy across the `A | B` cut: `EE M = H (rhoA M).IsHermitian.eigenvalues`.

`EE_eq_vonNeumann` identifies this with the paper's Eq. (3), `E(|ψ⟩_XY) = -tr(ρ_X log ρ_X)`,
through `vonNeumann ρ = -tr(ρ log ρ)` over Mathlib's `Matrix.IsHermitian.cfc`. The bridge
`trace_cfc` (`tr (f A) = ∑ f(eigenvalueᵢ)`) is ours: Mathlib has the functional calculus and
`trace_eq_sum_eigenvalues`, not the combination. `EE_eq_log_card_of_maxMixed` gives
`EE = log d` for a maximally mixed cut side of dimension `d`, the Bell pair being `d = 2`.

`Region` through `EntropyBounds` are the library layer; the rest is the worked example.

`SmoothEntropy` uses M9's `SubState.ball` from `PurifiedDist`, keeping Shannon entropy in
`Entropy`, pure states in `State`, and mixed states in `Mixed`. Its entropy primitives use
natural logarithms, with base-two forms derived by division by `log 2`. Extended real values
retain the infinities arising from zero rank or empty optimization sets. `maxEntropy` is
the paper's log-rank (Rényi-0) quantity; the distinct fidelity-based Rényi-1/2 entropy and
the inequality converting between them remain separate future work.

## Building

```bash
lake exe cache get
lake build
```

Toolchain `leanprover/lean4:v4.32.0`, with Mathlib and
[Physlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib) pinned by the lakefile. Regenerate
the root aggregator after adding a module with `lake exe mk_all --lib Arealaw --git`; CI checks
it stays in sync.

## Axiom audit

`Arealaw/Audit.lean` runs `#print axioms` on every headline result, and all report

```
depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
```

the three standard axioms of Lean and Mathlib: no `sorry`, no `native_decide`, no custom
axiom. CI fails on anything else.

## Relation to Physlib

Physlib, which absorbed Timeroot's Lean-QuantumInfo in 2026, is a dependency and carries the
broad theory: `HermitianMat` with continuous functional calculus, partial traces, relative
entropy, strong subadditivity, CPTP channels, the Generalized Quantum Stein's Lemma. On the
narrow overlap the definitions agree, their `Sᵥₙ = Hₛ ∘ spectrum` and `M Mᴴ` against our
`EE = H ∘ eigenvalues` and `rhoA`; both prove the spectrum-of-Kronecker fact Mathlib lacks
(`spectrum_prod` there) by the same simultaneous-diagonalization argument; and this layer is
`RCLike`-generic where theirs is fixed to `ℂ`. Physlib names relative-entropy additivity but
no von Neumann additivity equality; that is `EE_kronecker_add : EE (ψ₁ ⊗ₖ ψ₂) = EE ψ₁ + EE ψ₂`.
